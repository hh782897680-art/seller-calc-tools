## 3. Long description

### Version 1

Ecom Profit Tools is a free collection of browser-based calculators built for online sellers who want a clearer view of costs before making pricing or advertising decisions. Sellers can estimate transaction fees, marketplace profit, profit margin, return on investment, return on ad spend, and break-even scenarios using practical, editable inputs. Focused tools include a PayPal Fee Calculator for modeling payment deductions, a Shopify Fee Calculator for reviewing payment and applicable transaction-fee assumptions, and an Etsy Profit Calculator for considering product costs, delivery, listing fees, processing charges, and optional advertising assumptions across modeled orders.

The tools are designed for planning and education rather than accounting or financial advice. Calculator results are estimates, and users should confirm current platform, payment-provider, country, currency, and account terms before relying on a scenario. No account is required to use the current calculators, and calculations run in the browser.

### Version 2

Running an ecommerce business often means comparing costs that appear in different places: payment processing, marketplace charges, product and shipping expenses, and advertising performance. Ecom Profit Tools brings practical estimators together in one free website so sellers can explore a scenario before setting a price, offering a discount, or reviewing a campaign.

Users can start with dedicated Shopify, PayPal, or Etsy calculators, then use broader profit margin, ROI, ROAS, and break-even tools as their question changes. Inputs can be adjusted to reflect the seller's own assumptions instead of treating one published number as universal. The output is intended to support planning conversations and quick comparisons, not to replace statements, platform documentation, bookkeeping, tax guidance, or professional advice.

Every calculator result is an estimate. Fees and policies can vary by location, currency, account type, provider, and over time, so sellers should verify the terms relevant to their business.

### Version 3

Ecom Profit Tools offers free ecommerce calculators for sellers, marketers, and small business owners who need simple ways to model sales decisions. The site covers payment fees, marketplace profitability, margin, ROI, ROAS, and break-even analysis, with dedicated tools for common scenarios such as a PayPal payment, Shopify checkout fees, or a group of modeled Etsy orders.

A seller can enter assumptions, compare outcomes, and copy results into a planning note without creating an account. This can be useful when considering pricing changes, average order value, paid advertising, or the effect of channel fees on expected profit. The tools aim to make calculations easy to follow while keeping the seller responsible for choosing appropriate inputs.

Calculations are estimates for educational and planning purposes. They do not guarantee current fees or actual profitability and do not constitute financial, tax, accounting, or legal advice. Sellers should check applicable platform terms and their own records before making decisions.
